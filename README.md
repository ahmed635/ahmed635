**NamaSoft — Java Backend Engineer & Flutter Developer**  
📍 Cairo, Egypt · 🗓️ March 2024 – Present

Building the mobile-facing platform of a large-scale enterprise ERP (**Java 21, Spring Boot, Hibernate, MS SQL Server**) deployed at client organizations across Egypt and Saudi Arabia — owning features end to end, from domain model and API contract through to the Flutter clients that consume them.

- 🔌 **Backend APIs** — RESTful layer serving the customer and employee mobile apps: authentication, user management, sales and delivery documents, maintenance requests, customer visits, and HR self-service workflows.
- 🔐 **Auth & Security** — redesigned mobile authentication from session-based to **API Key**, with custom Spring Security filters, JWT, encrypted credential storage, role-based authorization, and biometric login.
- ⚡ **Real-Time & Notifications** — built a messaging platform over **WebSocket/STOMP** with authenticated handshake interception, plus a push service on **Firebase Cloud Messaging HTTP v1** using the OAuth2 service-account flow and a queued retry processor.
- 💳 **Payments & KitchenNet** — integrated **HyperPay** and related gateways; led *KitchenNet*, a white-label product line, as sole engineer on the standalone Spring Boot service and its Flutter client — shipped to **Google Play** and the **App Store**.
- 🧩 **ERP Modules & Performance** — HR, CRM, Point of Sale, Accounting, Supply Chain, Contracting, and Customer Service: geofenced electronic attendance, Bluetooth thermal receipt printing, application-level caching, and SQL/Hibernate tuning on high-traffic endpoints.
- 📱 **Cross-Platform Delivery** — own the Android and iOS release pipelines; delivered **three client brands** from a single Flutter codebase and led upgrades through Flutter 3.27, Android 15, and Play 16 KB page-size compliance.
